Currently, 85% of Texas residents live within a competitive electricity market. This means those residents have the ability to choose their electricity provider and plan. But that choice comes with ongoing responsibilities and consequences if not managed correctly. When you opt your electricity yourself, rates change, plans expire, and pricing structures can shift in ways that affect your bill.

Retail electricity providers (REP) like TXU and Gexa Energy offer a range of plans, but their roles typically end once service begins. They do not monitor the market on your behalf or transition you to more competitive plans as conditions change. Maintaining a competitive rate requires actively managing your contract, staying aware of pricing shifts, and making timely decisions about when to switch to avoid higher costs.

The Right Approach to Managing Your Electricity

Choosing between Energy Ogre and shopping directly with electricity providers comes down to whether you want to manage electricity yourself - or have it fully managed for you.

Shopping Directly with a Texas Electricity Provider Is Best If You:

  • Want to choose and manage your own plan
  • Are comfortable comparing electricity plans and EFLs
  • Have time to monitor your contract and shop the market 2-3 times per year
  • Don’t mind taking on the risk of pricing changes and renewals

Energy Ogre Is Best If You:

  • Want the lowest cost over time
  • Prefer a done-for-you solution
  • Don't want to track or manage contracts or rates
  • Value time, transparency, and peace of mind
