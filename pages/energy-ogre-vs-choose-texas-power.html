Compare All You Want…Then What?

Sites like Choose Texas Power are designed to simplify the shopping process by organizing available plans in one place. You can filter options, compare rates, and enroll with a provider. But those comparisons are based on generalized usage levels, simplified pricing, and the state of the electricity market at a single moment.

What they don't do is account for how electricity actually works in real life.

Your usage changes month to month. Plan pricing isn't linear. And the “best rate” today may not be the best rate even a few months from now. Once you enroll, the responsibility to re-enroll and switch plans is entirely on you.
