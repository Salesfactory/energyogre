Frequently Asked Questions

Power to Choose is the official electricity marketplace run by the Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT). It was created to give Texans a way to shop for electricity plans in the deregulated market. You can view offers from dozens of retail electricity providers (REPs), compare rates, and sign up directly with the provider you choose.

That said, it's important to understand that Power to Choose is a listing platform as opposed to a management service like Energy Ogre. It shows you available offers from a range of electricity providers, but it doesn't analyze your actual usage or help you determine the best available plan for your home.

Power to Choose is the official electricity marketplace run by the Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT) and includes a wide variety of plans, such as:

  • Fixed-rate plans: Lock in a rate for 12–36 months
  • Variable-rate plans: Rates change month to month
  • Prepaid plans: Pay-as-you-go electricity
  • Promotional plans: Include incentives like free nights/weekends, bill credits, or tiered pricing

While there are many options, most plans are structured around specific usage levels (like 500, 1000, or 2000 kWh). If your actual usage falls outside those ranges, your real cost per kWh can be very different from what's advertised.

The biggest risk is misunderstanding how pricing actually works. Many plans are designed to look competitive at certain usage levels but can become more expensive as your actual usage increases or falls outside those ranges.

Some common pitfalls include:

  • Bill credits that only apply at specific usage thresholds
  • Tiered pricing that penalizes being slightly above or below target usage
  • Rates advertised at averages that don't reflect your actual bill

Since Power to Choose doesn't tailor results to your personal usage or manage your plan over time like Energy Ogre, you're responsible for reading the fine print, estimating your usage, and monitoring your plan as rates and contracts change.

Power to Choose is primarily designed for residential users. While some providers may list small business plans, most commercial electricity needs require custom quotes and are handled outside the platform.

For businesses or higher-usage households, a managed approach like Energy Ogre can help ensure plans are evaluated based on actual usage rather than generic listings.

If you decide to self-manage your electricity service instead of having a service like Energy Ogre handle it for you, you'll need to:

  • Enter your ZIP code
  • Sort through hundreds of plans using filters like rate type and contract length
  • Compare available options and pricing structures
  • Click through to the provider's site to enroll

Where it gets more complex is everything that comes after. You're responsible for choosing a plan that actually fits your usage, tracking your contract end date, remembering to re-enroll before rates change, and checking the market regularly to see if a better option becomes available.

The platform doesn't calculate your expected bill, so selecting the right plan requires careful review of the Electricity Facts Label (EFL) and ongoing attention to your account over time.
