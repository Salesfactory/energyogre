In Texas’s competitive electricity market, Power to Choose is the official state-run marketplace for comparing electricity plans. Managed by the Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT), the site allows consumers to browse offers, filter by contract length or renewable content, and enroll in a plan.

However, it’s still a self-service platform, meaning you’re responsible for selecting the right plan, managing your contract moving forward, and renewing your contract or finding a new plan. To make matters more complicated, some providers upload intentionally misleading or confusing plans in hopes users sign up as a result. We call these plans “gimmick” or “bullseye” plans.

As the only unbiased, fully managed, end-to-end electricity service in Texas, Energy Ogre takes the stress out of managing your power. We serve as your advocate to continuously monitor the market, manage your account, and keep you on the best available plan.
